R6: the pages left after R5, in the same language: lists as cards, actions as glyph keys, every page its own picture.

Lists and actions

PROJECT (and USER, FM6 STORE, PHRASES): KNOB 2 the slot, KNOB 1 the action, ✓ = OCT+
TOOLS: KNOB 1 across, KNOB 2 down, OCT+ does it
MOTION: each recorded knob as a lane over the bar
MOD: KNOB 2 the route; 1 source, 3 destination, 4 amount

Rings over a picture

ARP: the notes it plays, climbing
SCL: its settings as a list (KNOB 2 the row, KNOB 1 the value, OCT+ the scales list); the octave under it
CHORD: its name, its notes on two octaves
PATTERN: the 64 steps as four bars of dots
SLICER: the gate it cuts, at its depth
FM6 EG: the operator's envelope as a curve page

Full screen

SCALES: KNOB 1 the family, KNOB 2 the scale
Drum STEP: KNOB 1 the step, KNOB 2 the lane, OCT+ sets the hit
A layer (GLO held): the keys' map as a sheet over the page

Screens of their own

NAME: KNOB 1 the place, KNOB 2 the letter
NEW SONG 1/2: key and tempo
NEW SONG 2/2: a role a track, Stage's columns
About: the name, the version, the credits, the QR
